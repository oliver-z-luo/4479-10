# Intent

The high-level intent for this home. Requirements and layout options should trace back to something here. If they can't, either they don't belong or this file is missing something.

## Who lives here

- Two adults, both working in software.
- Yuna, 2.5 years old. She learns very quickly when the environment is set up for her.
- A second child is likely. The home should be ready for that without a redesign.

## The space

- 1,098 sq ft condo, 3 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms, 8'10" ceilings, 53 sq ft balcony.
- Small for what we are asking of it, so every decision below is made under that constraint.
- This is a full renovation, so walls, doors and built-ins can change. Strata approval is required for these and is a known step.

## Principles

Proposed priority order: when two conflict, the earlier one wins.

1. **The environment teaches (Montessori).** The home is set up so Yuna can do things herself.
   - Her own things are at her height and within her reach.
   - A few visible choices, rotated, rather than everything out at once.
   - Everything has a place she can return it to.
   - Daily routines (dressing, washing, helping in the kitchen) are set up for her to take part in.
2. **No TV, minimal screens.** We want her to explore the world and to be bored. No room is organized around a screen.
3. **Every room earns its space.** Rooms serve more than one purpose wherever possible. Nothing is single-use if it can be shared.
4. **Ready for a second child.** The layout absorbs a baby without displacing what already works.
5. **Parents can work.** At least one permanent workspace with storage, for one adult. A second, temporary or permanent, is secondary: only if space or smart design allows.
6. **We host up to 8 comfortably, household included.** Anyone beyond 8 makes do on the floor. For family and for friends with children: adults can join the children's play while still talking among themselves, so seating and play stay within sight and earshot.

### Secondary

- Easy to clean.
- Easy to maintain.
- Built-in or custom storage, placed where it has a purpose (starting with Yuna's room).

## Decided

Room names match the labels on the floor plan.

- **Office Bedroom** is Yuna's bedroom.
- **Bedroom** is primarily a playroom, and occasionally a guest room (a futon or similar).
- **When a second child arrives, Bedroom becomes theirs alone.** No guests and no entertaining in it. It stays that way until the two children can sleep in the same room.
- **Storage** (the room beside the kitchen) is the permanent office: the workspace in principle 5. It is ventilated by a fan with the door kept open. There is no other space for an office.
- **Visits end by 9 PM,** so hosting does not conflict with Yuna's sleep.
- **No TV** anywhere in the home.

## Under consideration

- **Opening the wall between Office Bedroom and Bedroom,** so play can spread across both rooms without a barrier. Example: a race track too big for one room. Options:
  - **Double doors** (hinged, solid). About a 5' opening. Best sound separation, and the wall beside them stays usable for storage. Open doors take up floor space.
  - **Pocket doors.** About a 4' opening, with no door swing. Weaker on sound, and the hollow wall cannot take built-ins.
  - **Sliding wall or partition.** Up to nearly the full 9' width, the most open of the three. Weakest on sound unless it is an acoustic system.

## To discuss

- **Where children play during a visit, and how adults stay close to them.** Trade-offs not yet weighed. Options so far:
  - Children play in Bedroom, with a second adult seating area in there so adults can chat while watching them.
  - The play area is in the living room instead.
  - A second door into Bedroom from the living room.

## Not doing

- A media wall or a living room arranged around a screen.
- A dedicated guest room.
- A bedroom used as an office.
